# Teacher jurisdictions

Each model grants a capability, not global card authority. A model may change the working hypothesis; it may not override Product Charter, Constitution, or verified RWS visual facts.

## Nichols — archetypal and visual amplification

Activate for reliable RWS motifs, Major movement, sequence/progression, projection, polarity, symbolic amplification, or an archetype-to-human bridge. Keep the chain visible detail → motif/relation/movement → candidate amplification → warrant → polarity or alternative → spread/neighbors → reality judgement with agency and uncertainty. Nichols' comparative Marseille or cultural observations are method material, not replacement RWS facts.

## Greer — actual reversals

Activate only for an explicitly supplied actual reversal. Start from the upright baseline, then test mechanism candidates such as blocked, projected, delayed, internalized, excess, misuse, return, correction, or redirection against question, position, neighbors, background, and any supplied feedback. A reversal is not an automatic opposite or bad outcome. `unspecified` does not enter this branch.

## Dawn — Court ontology

Activate for Court-heavy spreads or person/role questions. Test whether a Court is an external person, the querent's self-aspect, a relationship role, or a behavior pattern before weak physical identifiers. Multiple Courts may describe multiple people, one person across domains, a relationship structure, or internal organization. Court count is not person count; use rank, element, position, background, and observable behavior.

## Daniel — spread organization and conditional correspondence

Activate for question framing, spread purpose/positions, subjective/objective separation, image-to-reality bridging, suit/number structure, or optional correspondence. Named positions bind interpretation; no-position left-to-right is only a candidate axis. Correspondences are conditional candidates, never weights or identity facts. Event questions remain event-level as well as psychological where the cards support that.

## Tarot Dictionary — late manifestation reference

Activate only after a holistic hypothesis exists and a precise lookup can change the judgment. Query card, orientation, domain, and reason through the release's exact Dictionary units; unsupported entries fail closed. Record `support`, `refine`, `challenge`, `alternative`, or `no_change`, then return to the whole spread and observed reality. The Dictionary proposes possible manifestations, not specific facts, and does not own worldview, spread organization, Court ontology, or reversal mechanism.
