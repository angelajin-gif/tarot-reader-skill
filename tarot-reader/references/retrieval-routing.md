# Conditional retrieval routing

Retrieval is evidence for a live holistic hypothesis, not the reading engine. Form the spread gestalt first, then query only a capability whose evidence can change the judgment. Do not retrieve every teacher for every card, do not impose a teacher citation quota, and do not use one Nichols trace as a substitute for a better-fitting source.

| Capability | Trigger | Query route | Required effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daniel card narrative | ordinary RWS upright/base card needs author image narrative, process/tension, or reality bridge; unspecified direction may use a labelled baseline | `scripts/query_teacher_evidence.rb --teacher daniel [--orientation upright|unspecified]` | 78-card author narrative, meaning candidates, process/tension, and examples remain author candidates; unspecified never becomes upright |
| Dawn Court ontology | Court-heavy spread, person/role question, or multiple-Court tension | `--teacher dawn` or `--capability dawn_rank_function` | Court/rank evidence changes role, behavior, or relationship structure conditionally |
| Greer reversal mechanism | at least one actual reversed card | `--teacher greer --orientation reversed` | 70 card-specific units may supply a context-tested mechanism; eight Page/Knight gaps return an explicit `coverage_fallback` general framework with unselected candidates and general-method provenance, never a card-specific reversal conclusion |
| Nichols amplification | a live hypothesis and an explicitly selected, card-bound Major evidence route | `--teacher nichols --hypothesis TEXT --reason TEXT --focus TEXT --nichols-trigger TYPE --reason-anchor unit:FIELD --focus-anchor unit:FIELD\|visual:FIELD` | trigger must be `motif`, `progression`, `projection`, `polarity`, or `archetypal_amplification`; the reason anchor must be an existing Nichols field permitted for that trigger, and the focus anchor must be a non-empty field on the selected unit or frozen visual packet |
| Dictionary manifestation | after a whole-spread hypothesis, the user supplies an observed circumstance and asks how a concrete work/relationship/domain manifestation should be checked; a relevant reviewed card/orientation/domain unit exists | `scripts/query_dictionary_reference.rb` or `--teacher dictionary` | use the reviewed unit to frame what to verify; record `support`, `refine`, `challenge`, `alternative`, or `no_change`; no observed circumstance or unsupported entry is a stop, not an invitation to invent a substitute |

Every query record keeps `hypothesis_before_retrieval`, `retrieval_reason`, source unit and provenance, returned effect, and `hypothesis_after_retrieval` in the reader's internal evidence chain. These fields are not exposed as an execution log. When a teacher changes user-visible judgment, mention the teacher, unit or concept, page/excerpt provenance, operation, and warrant beside that conclusion. When it makes no change, do not add a decorative citation.

For an explicitly requested concrete manifestation, do not stop at a vivid visual-to-story leap if a reviewed Dictionary unit can test it. The late check may narrow or challenge the initial hypothesis; the reviewed unit's actual wording is the ceiling on source-attributed specificity. An image's object count does not become a real-world allocation, and neither image nor Dictionary establishes a hidden actor, covert resource transfer, or actual workplace decision. In an observed case, compare the user's reported gaps with the Dictionary's actual domain statement; do not import a secrecy, theft, or resource-movement story from a different Daniel meaning candidate or the card picture. If the Dictionary only supports task capacity or short-versus-long risk, report precisely that effect and leave budget, permission, and responsibility checks attributed to the user's observations or to a clearly labelled general checklist. When no such facts were supplied, lead with “the concrete arrangement is not knowable from these cards” and turn possibilities into questions to verify, not a likely event narrative. If the Dictionary has no supported entry for the needed card/orientation/domain, state that coverage gap and keep any remaining inference conditional. Do not use Dictionary to decide a diagnosis, legal result, or investment action, even if a domain label appears relevant.

The unified Teacher Evidence query returns only reviewed, hash-bound, provenance-complete units. It rejects unknown or ambiguous selectors, unsupported orientations, teacher/capability mismatch, missing units, unreviewed content, duplicate IDs, hash drift, and absent source links. It never returns raw OCR, RWS visual packets, color, symbolic readings, review copies, or a card-meaning fallback.

For Nichols, the CLI treats `hypothesis`, `reason`, and `focus` as caller-supplied context text. Non-empty text is required, but deterministic code does not claim to validate its meaning. The structured trigger and field selectors establish which reviewed Nichols and frozen visual fields were selected for the lookup; the returned `evidence_detail.retrieval_selection` preserves those three validated selectors for audit. It does not echo the free text or prove that the prose accurately describes those fields. Missing or unsupported selections fail closed.
